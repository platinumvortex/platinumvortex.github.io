What it does

  • Two lights on every listing: does it fit you, and how good are your chances of getting it
  • Your real commute time and the electricity bill for a flat that size, not just the rent
  • A comparison table, a map, and a side by side view of your saved flats
  • A ready-made cover letter and the landlord's contact details

Your data stays on your computer

FlatDesk has no server, no account and no analytics. Your profile and saved flats live in your own browser and are never uploaded. See the privacy policy for exactly what is sent where.
