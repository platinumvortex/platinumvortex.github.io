Privacy policy

Your CV and your job search stay on your computer. This page says exactly what leaves it, and to whom.

Last updated: 29 September 2026

No server of our own

JobDesk has no backend, no account and no analytics. Your profile, the information read from your CV, your saved jobs, their status and your settings are stored locally in your browser and are never uploaded to us.

Your CV

When you add a CV, the file is read on your computer. JobDesk keeps only what it reads from it (roles, education, skills and languages) and the file name. It does not store or send the file itself. Where Chrome offers its built-in on-device AI, JobDesk may use it to read the CV more accurately; this also runs on your computer.

Pages JobDesk reads

JobDesk reads the job listing you open on jobs.ch, jobup.ch, Indeed and LinkedIn, so it can show the JobDesk panel next to it. If you switch on "Also on company career sites" in Settings, it can also read job pages on other websites after Chrome asks for your permission. It does not read other pages you visit.

Public services

To work out commutes and company ratings, JobDesk sends only the information each service needs:

ServiceWhat is sentWhen
transport.opendata.chYour home address and the job's locationTo calculate a public transport commute, when you open or save a job
api3.geo.admin.chWhat you type in the address field, and your home and workplace addressesWhile you enter your address, and for bike or car commutes
routing.openstreetmap.deThe coordinates of your home and of the workplaceFor bike or car commutes, if you choose them
kununu.com, indeed.comThe employer's nameTo look up the public company rating of a job you saved

Links such as "Search on jobs.ch" open the job board with a search term only when you click them.

Backups and exports

Backups and the RAV list are files you create yourself. They are saved where you choose and are not sent anywhere by JobDesk.

What we never do

  • We do not sell or share your data.
  • We do not use your data for anything other than helping you with your job search.
  • We do not use your data to assess creditworthiness or for lending.
  • We do not load or run code from remote servers.

Deleting your data

Remove a job in the dashboard to delete it. Removing the extension from Chrome deletes everything JobDesk has stored.
